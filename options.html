Pause when I type Pause video when typing in text fields (search, comments, chat)

Works with YouTube, Twitch, Netflix, HTML5 video / audio including embeds (all frames). No data collection.

Privacy Policy

PauseSpring collects nothing. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no network requests.

On your device only

Your settings are saved in the browser and never sent anywhere.

Typing and page content

Typing detection runs locally to trigger a pause and is immediately discarded. Page content is never read or transmitted.

Permissions

Tabs: detect tab switches. Storage: save settings. All URLs: run the pauser on any site, including embeds.
